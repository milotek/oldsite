About

Who

Milo Tekchandani, 19, London. I go by milo tek.

I'm a Software Engineer (Apprentice) at Google, on the Search app for mobile. Backend and infrastructure. Before that: Hills Road Sixth Form College (Computer Science, Maths, English, and an EPQ), then the Makers bootcamp. I was building things at weekends the whole way through.

I like systems that can be rebuilt from a file, tools that compose instead of enclose, and software that looks like somebody cared. I dislike dependency sprawl and configuration that only exists because someone clicked something once.

Outside work: Counter-Strike, Godot, old Minecraft, drawing, and a home server that hosts more than it strictly needs to.
