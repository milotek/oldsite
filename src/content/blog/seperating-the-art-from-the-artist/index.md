## """FURRY MUSICIANS"""
Who is the best furry musician? Why are they all mid? Frankly, the *actual* question at hand is who gives a flying fuck?

In my opinion, making the decision you to lean into the "**furry** musician" label to me just means you want to pedal to a niche audience because the competition is scarce - and suddenly the bar for decent music is lower. No, it does not make your music any better in the grand scheme of things.

There are actually only 3 (three) good """FURRY MUSICIANS""":
1. Car Seat Headrest
2. xaev
3. Renard

And I never cared about who or what they were. It might be a little rude to be ignorant to part of an artist's identity, but I like to seperate the art from the artist. It obviously influences their art, but I like to listen to music, and not care about the individual. You can apply this to Kanye.

I said my peace. Goodbye. What useless information!
