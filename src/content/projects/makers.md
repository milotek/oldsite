Before Google I did the Makers software engineering bootcamp. Two of the group projects are public.

- **makersbnb**: Flask with a repository pattern over Postgres, Jinja templates, and a test suite in pytest and Playwright. Built test-first with a team of six.
- **acebook**: Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, Flyway migrations, Auth0 for sign-in, Selenium and JUnit for tests. A team of nine, and my share was the smaller one.

Group work, so the commit history says who did what.
