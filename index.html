Lockyn unreleased2024 to now

A study app for GCSE and sixth form students. Timers, streaks, per-topic summaries, and a UI I'm actually proud of.

  • typescript
  • react native
  • expo
  • firebase
  • Front end in React Native, so it runs on iOS, Android, web and desktop off one codebase.
  • Firebase on the back end: auth, a non-relational store, and no server for me to babysit.
  • Started life as my A level Computer Science NEA, which is why it was gated on results day.
  • Results day has been and gone. Release date is "when it's good", which I'm aware is what everyone says.

ghostty~/projects/petfeeder

AutoPet Feeder shipped2024

A fully automated, slightly luxurious pet feeder, designed and built to my neighbours' spec. Real client, real deadline, real pet with opinions.

  • raspberry pi
  • javascript
  • servo
  • cad
  • 3d printing
  • oak
  • Raspberry Pi driving a servo, touchscreen on the front, USB-C for power because nobody has the right barrel jack.
  • ABS shell, 3D printed, with a solid oak lid. Went through a cardboard prototype and about nine CAD revisions first.
  • The full design document (client interviews, prototypes, evaluation, price list) is public. It's long.

ghostty~/projects/ezcals

EZcals rewriting2024 to 2025

A calorie tracker for people who want to log a meal in as few taps as physically possible and then get on with their day.

  • swift
  • swiftui
  • react native
  • supabase
  • postgres
  • Version one was pure Swift and SwiftUI. Lovely to write, iOS only.
  • Rewritten in React Native in 2025 for Android and web, with a self-hosted Supabase (so, Postgres) behind it.
  • Logs calories, protein, fat and the rest. The point was speed, not a feature list.
