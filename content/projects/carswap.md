Add-on cars in GTA V normally live outside the game's systems: you can drive them and that's it. CarSwap reads a text file of `original:replacement` pairs and swaps the models at load, so the game keeps treating them as the stock car and everything that stock cars can do keeps working.

It's 121 lines of Lua for the Stand menu and it is the single most useful stupid thing I have written.
