title: CarSwap
summary: A Lua script for the Stand menu that lets add-on cars behave like official GTA Online vehicles, garage, saving, mods and all.
year: 2025
order: 15
tags: [Lua, GTA V]
repo: https://github.com/milotek/StandLua-CarSwap
---
It reads a text file of `original:addon` pairs and swaps the model on the fly, so a Pegassi Reaper can become a Lamborghini Aventador add-on while the game keeps treating it as the Reaper. Colours and tuning carry across as far as the replaced vehicle allows.
