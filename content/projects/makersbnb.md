An Airbnb clone built in a week at Makers with five other people. Flask and Basecoat UI on the front, Postgres behind, pytest and Playwright for the tests. Users list spaces, request bookings, approve or reject them.

Test-driven from the first commit, which was the point of the exercise. My bits were mostly the listings flow and the database layer.[^1]
