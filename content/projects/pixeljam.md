My game studio. The org bio says "the world's worst video games?" and I stand by the question mark.

**ROXOR** is a Roblox base project for games grounded in realism: a proper character controller, melee and gun systems, a downed and revive state, ragdolls, and gore you can turn off. Valve's games are the obvious influence. **neosource** is Fragsurf ported to Unity 2022.3 and URP 14 with a Linux editor, so surf and bhop can be built on this decade's tooling. **Schism**, a co-op horror roguelike on Roblox, is the hobby game that all this infrastructure was originally for.[^1]
