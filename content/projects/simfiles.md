A pack of modcharts for notITG. Every chart is mine.

I like rhythm games and I like Lua, and modcharting turns out to be a properly good way to learn GLSL, because a shader that's wrong is instantly, visibly wrong in time with the music.

Three charts so far. I don't own a DDR pad, which probably shows.
