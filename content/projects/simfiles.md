Modcharting is writing Lua that moves the arrows around in time with the song, and shaders for when moving them is not enough. I picked it up because I like rhythm games and I like Lua, and it turns out to be a very direct way to learn GLSL.

Three charts so far, varying wildly in quality. I do not own a dance pad, which probably shows.
