The question the project asked was whether you can design a platformer where the accessibility options are the design rather than a menu you open after the fact. Remapping, difficulty, timing windows, contrast, all decided before the levels were.

Built in Godot with a mix of C# and GDScript, over about six months of weekends during the first year of my A levels. Development ran on user feedback in a loop, which is the part that actually taught me something.

The character concepts on the [art page](/art/) are from this.
