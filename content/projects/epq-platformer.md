A platforming and exploration game with accessibility as the actual design constraint rather than an options menu bolted on at the end.

Six months of weekends during the first year of my A levels, written for my Extended Project Qualification, so it came with a research question, user testing rounds and a dissertation arguing about the results.

Godot, C# and GDScript. I drew the character concepts myself, which you can tell.
