The EPQ question was how far you can push accessibility in a platformer when it's designed in from day one. Godot 3.5, C# and GDScript, iterated on tester feedback in a loop, during the first year of my A levels.

The repo is an early snapshot; the finished build never made it into source control (lesson learnt). The concept art for the two leads survived, though.
