My Extended Project Qualification, titled *An accessible platforming and exploration themed video game*. Godot, GDScript and C#, built over about six months of weekends during the first year of my A levels.

The premise was that platformers gate you on reaction time and precision by default, and almost none of that gating is load-bearing. So: remappable everything, adjustable timing windows, no required precision inputs, readable contrast.

I ran it as an iterative loop with real players rather than designing it all up front, which is the only reason the accessibility work is any good. I also drew the character concepts, which you can see above and in the [drawings](/art/).
