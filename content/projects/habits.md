Kotlin and Ktor, one SQLite file, and a page designed to be looked at from across a room rather than held. It ships as a Nix package and a module, so putting it on the kiosk is one line rather than an afternoon of Gradle.

Green squares are a genuinely good interface for "did you do the thing", and I wanted them for something other than commits.
