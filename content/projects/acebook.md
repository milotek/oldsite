Another Makers team project, this time in Java. Spring Boot with Thymeleaf templates, Flyway managing the Postgres schema, Auth0 and Spring Security for sign-up and sign-in, Lombok to keep the boilerplate down.

Testing is JUnit for units and Selenium through Chromedriver for the end-to-end flows.
