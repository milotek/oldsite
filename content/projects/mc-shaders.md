Shaders that put the Legacy Console Edition lighting back into Minecraft Java.

Written against 1.6.4 and ShadersModCore, which is a mod from 2013 that you have to hunt down, because I play old versions and I wanted the exact fog and the exact torch falloff from a console I no longer own.

Works on Iris and OptiFine too. [Video preview](https://youtu.be/EzzUaDSsb3Q).
