Built as my A level Computer Science NEA. It runs on iOS and Android, with web and desktop builds from the same codebase.

The pitch: log a study session, set a daily goal, keep a streak, and see where your screen time went. Onboarding asks what you're there for and shapes the home screen around it.

Release was blocked until after results day, and it's still unreleased. The source is private for now.
