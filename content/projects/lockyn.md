Study timer, goals and screen-time insight for students, built to run on iOS, Android, web and desktop off one codebase.

React Native and TypeScript on the front, Firebase behind it. It came out of my A Level computer science NEA and then kept going well past what the coursework needed.

The repo is private and it never shipped. The screenshots are the 2024 build.
