Drop it in a folder and every unanchored part in there starts making the right noise when it hits something. Brick sounds like brick, wood sounds like wood.

Volume scales off impact speed, soft and hard impacts get different samples, and any object can override its own material, so a bouncy ball can sound like a cartoon spring.

Ships with Source engine sounds by default, because Valve got there first and nobody has beaten them since.
