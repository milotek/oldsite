Drop it on a folder of unanchored parts and they start sounding like what they're made of. Brick sounds like brick, metal like metal, with a soft or hard hit chosen by impact speed and the volume scaled to match.

It uses Source engine sounds by default, supports per-object overrides (cartoon spring for a bouncy ball) and can emit particles on impact. It's primitive and the README says so.
