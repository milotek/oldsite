Dedicated servers on demand, a match lifecycle, and the plumbing to get ten people onto the right box. Built on MatchZy, MetaMod, CounterStrikeSharp and Agones for the fleet side.

Private while it's half-finished, and the domain is still parked. It'll be public when it's worth showing.
