Shaders that make modern Minecraft look like the Xbox 360 edition you played at a sleepover in 2013. Written for 1.6.4 and the ancient ShadersModCore, but they run on OptiFine and Iris too.

Quick, shitty, and exactly the vibe I wanted.[^1] Install is: download the zip, drop it in shaderpacks, profit.
