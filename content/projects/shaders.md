Shaders that make Minecraft Java look like the Xbox 360 edition. Warm lighting, that specific washed-out sky, the lot.

Written against 1.6.4 with ShadersModCore, which is the mod everyone used before OptiFine shipped shader support. Works on Iris and OptiFine too.

Entirely nostalgia driven. No apologies.
