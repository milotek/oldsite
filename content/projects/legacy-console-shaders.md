Tested on 1.6.4 with ShadersModCore, which is the mod you needed for shaders before OptiFine shipped its own support. It should run on anything with OptiFine or Iris too.

Drop the zip in `shaderpacks` and that's it. There's a video if you'd rather see it than take my word for it.
