An automated pet feeder built to a real client's spec. The client was my neighbours, and their cat is the actual end user.

Raspberry Pi driving a servo, a touchscreen for setting schedules, USB-C so it can be plugged in by someone who does not care how it works. Shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak finish, because it has to live in a kitchen.

The full design document is up there: client interviews, prototypes, testing and an honest evaluation of what I got wrong.
