An IoT pet feeder designed and built to a client specification. The client was my neighbours, which sounds like a joke until you've had a design review with them.

Raspberry Pi driving a servo, a touchscreen for the interface, USB-C in so anyone can power it. The shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak wood finish, because a thing that lives in someone's kitchen has to look like it belongs there.

I did the full loop on this one: client spec, cardboard prototypes, feedback, CAD, print, wire, write, evaluate. Getting told your first prototype is too big by the person who has to live with it is a better lesson than any brief.
