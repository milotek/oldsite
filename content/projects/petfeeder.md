My Product Design NEA. The clients were my neighbours, the brief was a fully automated feeder, and the result is a Pi driving a servo behind a touchscreen, powered over USB-C.

The shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak finish. The software started from rebeccathedev's Raspberry Pi cat feeder and was reworked for this build. The full design document (client spec, prototypes, evaluation) is linked below.
