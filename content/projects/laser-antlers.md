Platforming and exploration with accessibility as the actual design constraint rather than a settings menu bolted on at the end. Made in Godot, weekends across the first year of my A Levels.

The whole thing ran on a feedback loop: build a bit, hand it to someone, watch them struggle, change it. That's most of what the EPQ taught me.

The moose is called Laser Antlers. He's on the front page of this site.
