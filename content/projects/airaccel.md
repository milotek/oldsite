Surf and bhop have been stuck behind Source engine servers and a mechanically brutal learning curve for twenty years. Most people bounce off before it clicks.

AIRACCEL puts the movement in a browser tab so there's nothing to install and nothing to connect to. Mapping goes through Graybox, which is open source.

Ours is a fork under Pixeljam Studios. The original is Fragsurf's.
