Bunnyhop and surf have survived for twenty years on community goodwill and almost nothing else. The problem was never the movement, it's that finding a server means owning Counter-Strike, opening a server browser, and knowing what you're looking at.

AIRACCEL is that movement in a browser tab. Click a link, you're in.

The second half is tooling for the people making maps, because a movement game with no map pipeline dies the moment its mappers get bored.

Pixeljam Studios project, alongside Graybox and the Fragsurf lineage.
