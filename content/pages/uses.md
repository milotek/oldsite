What I actually work with, as declared in [nixeljam](https://github.com/milotek/nixeljam).

**Desktop.** NixOS with Hyprland, waybar, tofi, hyprlock and swaync. Everything themed through Stylix from one base16 scheme: Catppuccin Mocha with #ffbdbd in the accent slot.

**Terminal.** Ghostty, zsh with starship, zellij, eza, fzf and zoxide. MesloLGS Nerd Font at 12pt.

**Editor.** Neovim, configured declaratively with nvf. lazygit for git.

**Notes.** Obsidian. The posts here are written in the same vault.

**Servers.** A mini PC at home and a VPS, both NixOS. Caddy in front of copyparty, Gitea, AdGuard Home and Glance, with Tailscale for admin access and sops for secrets.

**Making things.** Blender and Godot on the PC. An iPad Pro with an Apple Pencil and Procreate for drawing.

**Work.** A Mac, with the same Ghostty and zsh setup ported across.
