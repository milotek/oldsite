A pomodoro shaped study app, built because my mate Lukas and I could not sit still for one hour of revision
between the two of us.

The free ones we tried were too rigid and the paid ones wanted a subscription for a timer. Lockyn does the
25 and 5 blocks, but you can set a different schedule per weekday, see where the time actually went, and
line your breaks up with friends so nobody's sat on their own waiting.

React Native and TypeScript on the front, Firebase behind it. It began as my A level NEA and kept going
after the coursework was marked. Not released yet.
