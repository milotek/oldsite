Every Roblox project we start needs the same 80%: a character controller that feels right, melee and guns,
health and stamina with a downed and revive state, ragdolls on death, an inventory and hotbar, a settings
page. Roxor is that 80%, written once.

The aesthetic target is Source. Grounded, physical, readable, with characters that flinch when you shoot
them. Valve build for one specific kind of game very deliberately, and so do we.
