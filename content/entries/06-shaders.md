Minecraft Legacy Console Edition had a look: heavy lighting, that specific fog, slightly cheap bloom. This
puts it back.

Written in GLSL for ShadersModCore on 1.6.4, which is the version I actually play, from before OptiFine
shipped shader support at all. It works fine on Iris and modern OptiFine too.

Small pack, not clever. It just gets the childhood right.
