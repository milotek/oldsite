Surf and bhop have two problems. Finding a server in CS or Garry's Mod is unintuitive, and once you're in,
the movement is hard enough that most people quit before it clicks.

AIRACCEL goes at the first one and softens the second. It runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install
and nothing to connect to. You click a link and you're sliding down a ramp badly.

TypeScript, Pixeljam Studios. Maps come out of Graybox, the open source editor built for Fragsurf.
