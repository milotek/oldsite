My Extended Project Qualification: a platforming and exploration game designed around accessibility from the
first sketch instead of having it bolted on at the end. Everything remappable, nothing that needs two hands
at once, no timing window you can't widen.

Built in Godot with C# and GDScript, over six months of weekends during the first year of my A levels,
rewriting chunks of it every time somebody played it in front of me and got stuck somewhere I hadn't
predicted.

The concept art is mine too. There's more of it on the [art page](../../art/).
