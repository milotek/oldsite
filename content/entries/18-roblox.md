My first properly shipped game was written in Lua on Roblox, and it picked up 15,000 unique players in its
first three months. I've been chasing that feeling ever since, with mixed success.

Since then there's been Schism, a co-op survival horror roguelike we mess about with under Pixeljam, and
CollisionSounds, a module that gives every unanchored part material correct impact sounds using Valve's
Source effects. That one's public and people actually use it.
