A team project: list a space, request a booking, approve or decline it, and never let two people book the
same night.

Flask and Postgres, Basecoat for the UI, Playwright driving the feature tests. Six people on one repo for a
week means the hard problem is never the code. It's agreeing what a Space actually is before anyone writes
the migration.

We built it test first, which sounds like the sort of thing you say at a bootcamp, and was the only reason
six of us could push to the same branch without the whole thing catching fire.
