Notes

Fragsurf is the closest thing surf and bunnyhop have to an open engine, and it was stuck on an old Unity with a render pipeline nobody ships anymore. We brought it forward.

It loads BSP maps at runtime, so CS:S and CS:GO geometry drops straight in. The point of the whole thing is that the modding path stays open, which is the part Source got right and everyone since has quietly closed off.
