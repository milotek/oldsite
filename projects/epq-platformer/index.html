Accessible 3D Platformer

2024 · game · finished

An accessibility-focused platformer, built in Godot over six months of weekends for my EPQ.

godotc#gdscriptaccessibility

Written in Godot with a mix of C# and GDScript, built across weekends during the first year of my A levels.

The research question was accessibility in platformers, so the design went out to real people, came back with complaints, and got changed. Repeatedly. That loop was the point of the project more than the game was.
