AutoPet Feeder

An automated pet feeder built to a real client's spec. My neighbours wanted one, so they got one.

Notes

A Raspberry Pi, a servo, a touchscreen and a hopper, wrapped in a 3D printed ABS shell with an oak top so it doesn't look like a science project in someone's kitchen. USB-C in, no batteries to forget about.

I did this one properly: client spec, cardboard mockups, three iterations, then a written evaluation at the end. The cardboard stage is the bit that saved me, because model one was the wrong shape and I found that out for the price of a box.
