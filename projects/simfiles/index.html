notITG charts

A pack of StepMania modcharts for notITG, with Lua mods and GLSL shaders. Three songs so far.

  • 2025
  • Lua
  • GLSL
  • Rhythm games

Rhythm games and Lua are two things I like, so modcharts were an obvious way to practise one and enjoy the other. Charts for xaev, asciicats and leroy, made in SMEditor, with the mods and shaders written by hand.

I don't own a DDR pad. Quality varies and the README admits it.
