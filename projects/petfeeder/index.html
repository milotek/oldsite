AutoPet Feeder

2024 · hardware · delivered

An automated pet feeder, built to a real client brief. Raspberry Pi, a servo, a 3D printed shell and an oak lid.

raspberry-pijavascriptcad3d-printingiot

A Product Design NEA, except the client was my neighbours and their dog, so the brief was real and the feedback was blunt.

Raspberry Pi driving a servo, JavaScript on top, a touchscreen on the front and USB-C for power. ABS shell printed after a run of cardboard prototypes, oak lid because the client's kitchen has wood in it.

The design document runs to a hundred-odd slides: client interviews, material research, iteration after iteration, a manufacturing spec and a peer review. Making the thing was maybe a fifth of the work.
