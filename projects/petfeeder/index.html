AutoPet Feeder

An automated pet feeder built to a real client's spec. My neighbours' cat has strong opinions about uptime.

  • raspberry pi
  • javascript
  • hardware
  • 3d printing

design document (pdf)

A real client with a real brief: my neighbours, who wanted the cat fed on a schedule without a grey plastic box sat in their kitchen looking like a grey plastic box.

A Raspberry Pi driving a servo, JavaScript on top, a touchscreen for setting times and USB-C so it charges off anything in the house. The shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak finish, which is the part everybody actually comments on.

It came with a full design document: client interviews, prototypes, iterations and an evaluation, because it was my Product Design coursework as well as a toy.
