Hardware and software, start to finish, for an actual client with actual requirements. My neighbours, but still.

Built for my Product Design NEA at school. A Raspberry Pi with a touchscreen drives a servo on a schedule. The shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak lid I made in the workshop. There's a full design document with the client spec, the prototypes and the evaluation.

The interesting part wasn't the code, it was finding out how much of the work is cardboard mockups and asking people what they meant.
