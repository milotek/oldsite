Six of us, one week, a Flask app where users list spaces, request bookings and manage reservations. Test-driven from the first commit because the alternative with six people touching the same routes is chaos.

Most of my time went on the booking flow and on making the thing look finished. Basecoat UI over Flask templates got us further than a component framework would have in the time we had.
