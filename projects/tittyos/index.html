You can call .backward() on a tensor for years without knowing how the graph gets built, and that bothered me. So I wrote one.

It does tensors with shapes and dtypes, the usual elementwise and reduction ops, and reverse-mode autodiff over them. It is nowhere near finished and it was never going to be. The point was the middle bit.

The name is a joke that I am now stuck with.
