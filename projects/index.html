AIRACCEL

2025 to now · in development

Bunnyhop and surf, in a browser tab. No Steam install, no server browser, no forty minutes of setup before you get to the fun part.

Babylon.js for rendering with Havok doing the physics, which is the hard bit: source-style air acceleration has to feel exactly right or the whole thing is pointless.

The other half of the problem is teaching it. Most people bounce off surf in the first two minutes, and that is a design failure rather than a player one.

AutoPet Feeder

2024 · delivered

An automated pet feeder built to a real client's spec. The client was my neighbours, and their cat is the actual end user.

Raspberry Pi driving a servo, a touchscreen for setting schedules, USB-C so it can be plugged in by someone who does not care how it works. Shell is 3D printed ABS with an oak finish, because it has to live in a kitchen.

The full design document is up there: client interviews, prototypes, testing and an honest evaluation of what I got wrong.
