A pomodoro-shaped studying app for students, built for my best friend Lukas and then for everyone with his problem.

The idea came from a livestream we both used for GCSE revision: a timer synced to real clock time, breaks at XX:25 and XX:55, so you could glance at any clock in the house and know where you were. The channel took it down and neither of us ever got the habit back.

So: work blocks that line up with the actual hour, a heads-up display of what you're supposed to be doing, per-day schedules, and stats that tell you when you're genuinely productive rather than when you felt busy.

iOS, Android, web and desktop off one React Native codebase, Firebase behind it. Started in SwiftUI, rewritten when I wanted it everywhere.

Started as my A level NEA and outgrew it. Still not released.
