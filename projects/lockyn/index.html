Project

Lockyn

A study and focus app for GCSE and sixth form students. Screen time, session timers, and a daily summary that doesn't nag.

Year
2024-2025
Role
Solo
Status
Built, unreleased
  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Firebase
  • Expo

Lockyn started as an A level NEA and turned into the thing I spent most of two years on. The pitch was simple: every study app I tried either punished you or gamified you into ignoring it, and I wanted one that just told you the truth about your day and got out of the way.

The frontend is React Native through Expo, so iOS, Android, web and desktop come off one codebase. Firebase does auth and storage. The interface went through more redesigns than the logic did.

It is finished enough to use and has never been released. It was tied to exam board rules while I was still a candidate, and by the time that lifted I had a job.
