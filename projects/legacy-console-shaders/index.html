Legacy Console Shaders

2026 · graphics · released

Makes Minecraft Java look like the Xbox 360 edition. Written for a 2013 mod, works in Iris.

glslminecraftshaders
RepoVideo

If you play old versions of Minecraft, and I do, OptiFine didn't ship with shader support yet. You need a mod from over a decade ago called ShadersModCore.

So these are written against that. Tested on 1.6.4, and they work fine on Iris and modern OptiFine too.

Re-live your childhood with these shitty shaders.
