legacy console shaders

Shaders that make Java Minecraft look like the Xbox 360 edition. Written for 1.6.4, obviously.

  • glsl
  • minecraft
  • shaders

source

Minecraft Legacy Console Edition had a look: heavy lighting, that specific fog, slightly cheap bloom. This puts it back.

Written in GLSL for ShadersModCore on 1.6.4, which is the version I actually play, from before OptiFine shipped shader support at all. It works fine on Iris and modern OptiFine too.

Small pack, not clever. It just gets the childhood right.
