Legacy Console Shaders

A GLSL shader pack that makes modern Minecraft look like the Xbox 360 edition, back when the fog was that particular shade of nothing.

I play old versions of Minecraft, which means 1.6.4 and a mod from 2013 called ShadersModCore, because OptiFine didn't ship shader support back then. If that sentence made sense to you, this pack is for you.

The goal was the Legacy Console Edition look rather than a realistic one: flat lighting, the specific fog distance, clouds that sit wrong. Getting something to look deliberately worse is harder than it sounds, because every default in a shader pipeline is pulling towards "nicer".

Works on Iris and OptiFine too. Download it, drop it in, done.
