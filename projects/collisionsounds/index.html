CollisionSounds

2025 · library · released

Roblox module that makes objects sound like what they're made of when they hit something.

luarobloxaudio
Repo

Brick hits the floor, you get a brick noise. Volume and pick between the soft and hard impact sample come from the speed of the collision.

Supports every material, takes per-object overrides so a bouncy ball can make cartoon spring noises, emits particles on impact, and deletes itself when the part goes to sleep.

Ships with Source engine sounds by default, because of course it does.
