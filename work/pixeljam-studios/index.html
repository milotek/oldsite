Studio

Pixeljam Studios

My game studio org. Engine work, movement systems and weapons, mostly for shooters that owe a lot to Source.

GitHub org

Pixeljam Studios is the org my game work lives under. Most of the repos in it are private, so this is a description rather than a link dump. Sorry.

The bulk of my commits go into two engine projects. One is a Roblox superset aimed at games grounded in realism, the other is a Unity port of a Source-style movement shooter. Different engines, same obsession.

What I've actually shipped in there

  • Source-style movement: ground and air takeover, auto-bhop, the whole feel.
  • CS:GO-style recoil, viewpunch and spread.
  • Bullet penetration.
  • View models, weapon sounds and per-weapon animator controllers.
  • A headless dev-session launcher, because waiting for an editor to boot 40 times a day is a tax.
  • Fixing the Unity 2022 build so it works on Linux, which is where I develop.

Valve is the reference point for all of it. Not the art direction, the streamlined developer experience and the way their games feel under your hands.
