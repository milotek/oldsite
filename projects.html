EZcals

A calorie tracker for people trying to lose or gain weight, built around one idea: logging a meal should take seconds, so you actually keep doing it.

  • iOS and Android. Logs calories, protein, fat and the rest.
  • Originally written entirely in Swift and SwiftUI.
  • Rewritten in React Native in 2025 so the Android half stopped being a promise.

 iot · built to a client's spec

AutoPet Feeder

A fully automated, slightly luxurious pet feeder. Designed and engineered for a real client, who happens to be my neighbours, which made the requirements meetings very short. =^)

  • Raspberry Pi, a servo, and JavaScript. Touchscreen on the front, USB-C power so it can live anywhere.
  • Custom software and hardware. The firmware started as a fork of rebeccathedev's petfeedd and grew from there.
  • ABS 3D printed shell with an oak wood finish, because it sits in a kitchen and kitchens deserve better than bare plastic.
  • Product Design NEA, with the full design document: client spec, prototypes, development, evaluation.
  • raspberry pi
  • javascript
  • servo
  • 3d printing
  • a level nea
  • delivered
